**Sound it is the soul of immersion.** Yet, for too long, 3D artists and sound designers have relied on _faking_ static soundscapes, looped effects and keyframed audio that never quite matches the physics on screen.

**pbrAudio** changes that. It is the first fully integrated, time-accurate acoustic suite that bridges the gap between 3D animation and audible reality. Whether you are shattering a cathedral, driving a car through a wall or simply walking a character through a reverberant hall, pbrAudio gives you the tools to _hear_ what the physics dictates.

# **Why pbrAudio?**

[![](https://pbraudio.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/0222-e1782226397787.png)](https://youtu.be/GTyKKMMKPTQ)

## ****Creative****

_Stop trying sound asset._  
Focus on the visual narrative while **pbrAudio** handles the sonic reality. Because the audio is physically simulated, it reacts to camera movements and scene changes automatically. Your sound design becomes deterministic, not guesswork.

## **VFX Artist**

_Let creativity meets automation._  
Give life to subtle interactions. The scrape of a chair, the fracture of glass slab, the timbre of debris impact, based on the object's material, all generated from the animation data you already have.

## ****Sound Designer****

_Full Control_.  
Whether a source moves behind a pillar, enters a tunnel, or flies overhead, the sound warps, occludes, and reverberates with physical accuracy. With **pbrAudio** you can fine-tune how materials absorb sound or how geometry reflects it. You are not just applying reverb; you are engineering the acoustic space.
